§E.3 Necessary bandwidth and emission designator

By 47 CFR §2.202(g) for frequency modulation, Bn = 2M + 2DK with M = 2420 Hz (the highest tone), D = {{DEV_MAX}} Hz (its peak deviation) and K = 1: Bn = {{BN}} kHz, emission designator {{BN_DESIG}}F2D (F: frequency modulation; 2: a single channel containing quantised information with a modulating subcarrier, the audio tones; D: data). The occupied bandwidth is smaller, {{OBW_MAX}} kHz at most (E.2), and both fit a standard 12.5 kHz FM channel. The handheld's frequency tolerance (about ±2.5 ppm, ±1.1 kHz at 440 MHz) moves the whole emission; the receiver searches ±2.5 kHz (section 1.7).
